Support

ExplainMe Support

For support with ExplainMe, email support@explainme.co.uk.

Please leave out private check-ins, reflection text and personal details about your child. If a screenshot helps, hide private content before sending it.

How to get help

When you contact support, include:

  • Your app version and iOS version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Any relevant screenshots or error messages

Topics we can help with

  • Signing in with Apple
  • Subscriptions and premium access
  • Family setup and child profiles
  • Check-ins, reflections, routines, and insights
  • Linked child-device pairing

Response times

We aim to respond to support requests within 3 business days.
