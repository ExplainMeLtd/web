# The remaining privacy publication decisions

The company number, postal address and Microsoft 365 support provider are confirmed. The owner reports informal advice from a lawyer friend; this sheet does not ask for a formal opinion or for the owner to write legal clauses. It identifies the decisions the public notice must accurately reflect.

## 1. Why the company is entitled to use each kind of information

The policy needs a specific legal reason for each purpose. A guardian accepting app terms or both people enabling AI does not automatically answer every question about a child's sensitive information.

| Actual purpose | Candidate to assess — not an approved company position |
|---|---|
| Guardian sign-in, account and purchased subscription | Performance of the guardian's contract for objectively necessary processing. Identify any actual accounting/legal obligation separately. |
| Child profiles, guided check-ins, saved/private/shared history and routines | Confirm the chosen basis for the child's own data. If legitimate interests is selected, document the child-focused necessity/balancing assessment; do not treat the guardian contract as the child's contract. If consent is selected, establish that the actual enrolment/choice flow supplies valid consent. |
| Optional Azure safety/generation and potentially sensitive child-entered content | Assess valid consent for optional AI and the separate explicit-consent or other applicable sensitive-data condition. Confirm age/capacity/guardian-authority arrangements, including children under 13. The existing two-person permission wording and records are evidence to assess, not a conclusion that all conditions are met. |
| Security, abuse prevention and report/appeal handling | Assess the specific legitimate interests and necessity. Reports can contain sensitive information or allegations; identify any additional legal condition needed. Do not presume every report is covered by a safeguarding exception. |
| Necessary product diagnostics/usage and support/rights correspondence | Confirm the purpose-specific grounds, minimisation and any device-access requirements. Identify real legal obligations only where applicable. |

**Needed:** the owner's/adviser's actual purpose-by-purpose choices and rationale, or changes required to make the processing match those choices. No unsupported grounds will be represented as settled in the public policy.

## 2. Where suppliers process data and the safeguards used

Known: Azure family storage is UK South; the AI deployments use Global processing, which can leave the UK; Microsoft 365 hosts support email; Apple, RevenueCat and GitHub also receive relevant information.

**Needed:** confirm which actual supplier contracts and UK transfer safeguards cover these flows and their relevant retention/monitoring arrangements. General provider documentation is helpful evidence, but does not establish every ExplainMe-specific contractual fact.

## 3. How long information is kept in practice

Known: Free history currently has a seven-day viewing limit; older history remains stored. Reports expire 90 days from submission. The current application telemetry tables retain 90 days, despite the workspace default being 30. Last-guardian departure follows the separate 90-day family-purge eligibility process. Enforcement markers have no automatic TTL; a separate seven-day database backup window exists.

Previously agreed proposals included rolling seven-day Free storage, 90-day post-Premium recovery/export, inactivity warnings/deletion, 30-day routine telemetry, case/email schedules and necessity reviews. Those proposals must not appear as implemented automatic deletion rules while the released product operates differently.

**Needed:** decide the justified current retention/rights handling and any required implementation/operational changes; confirm support-email, appeal/rights-case, administrative and exceptional incident schedules. The website task does not authorise silently altering service or mailbox retention.

## Practical next step

Review these three groups against the reconciled policy and linked source evidence. Publication approval should be for the resulting concrete policy, not for placeholders or a generic statement that everything is fine.
