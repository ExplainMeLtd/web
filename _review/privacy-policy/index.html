Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

ExplainMe Limited provides ExplainMe, which helps guardians and children manage family setup, check-ins, reflections, routines and shared insights. Eligible linked-child profiles can use guardian-enabled Teen Trust, including EM, an optional AI-assisted reflection feature.

For privacy questions, contact our monitored support mailbox at support@explainme.co.uk.

What data we collect

  • Account information, such as your name and email address
  • Family and child profile information that you enter in the app
  • Check-ins, reflections, routines, and related content you create
  • Subscription and entitlement information related to premium access
  • Usage and diagnostic data, including product analytics and technical telemetry
  • App and device identifiers used for app functionality, linked-device flows, and analytics

How we use data

  • To provide the app’s core functionality
  • To manage family accounts, child profiles, and linked child-device access
  • To process and restore premium subscription access
  • To generate insights and summaries from app activity
  • To monitor reliability, diagnose issues, and improve the product
  • To record AI permission choices, investigate reports and enforce restrictions on abusive or unsafe use

Services we use

ExplainMe uses third-party service providers to help operate the app, including:

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchase processing, and TestFlight distribution
  • RevenueCat, for subscription management and entitlement handling
  • Microsoft Azure, for backend hosting, data storage, application monitoring, AI safety checks and EM generation

Teen Trust, EM and AI permission

When you use EM, selected check-in choices and words you choose to enter are sent to ExplainMe and Microsoft Azure for AI safety checks and generated reflections or suggestions. This includes optional talk-through answers, clarification answers and requests for wording help. EM is AI, not a person, medical professional or emergency service.

Current guardian permission for the child and permission on the linked device are required before these AI requests. Existing Teen Trust settings do not count as permission. The guardian can review or withdraw permission in the child’s profile settings. The teen can decline before entering EM or withdraw permission in linked-device Settings > AI permission. Declining or withdrawing prevents future EM AI requests covered by that permission; it cannot recall information already sent with permission. Declining does not prevent access to reporting, saved check-ins or available support options.

ExplainMe does not save optional talk-through answers or wording-help drafts as a conversation transcript. Information you explicitly save as a check-in or reflection is different: private check-ins may sync to the child’s ExplainMe account. AI permission alone does not make them visible to guardians. A teen can preview and explicitly share a check-in through the app’s sharing flow. Showing a safety-support screen does not automatically contact or notify a guardian.

Microsoft’s service-specific processing and safety-monitoring arrangements also apply to AI requests. See Microsoft’s information about Azure AI data handling. Do not assume that withdrawing permission or deleting app data recalls a request already processed by a service provider.

Reporting a problem

A linked child can report a problem from the check-in flow, saved history or linked-device Settings without an email account or AI permission. We receive the selected reason, any report details entered, and identifiers for the family, child profile and linked device so authorised support staff can investigate. A displayed EM response is included only when the person reporting explicitly chooses to attach it. Private answers and conversation text are not attached automatically.

Reports are kept separately from guardian-visible check-ins. Sending a report does not share the check-in with guardians. Authorised staff can access the submitted report, record an investigation note and mark it as under review or resolved. Access and administrative actions are audited. Automated queue reminders do not include the report’s text.

Reports are not continuously monitored emergency support. Reporting does not call anyone or send an email from the child’s device. If a report concerns someone in the family, it is not automatically forwarded to that person.

Abuse prevention and account restrictions

Authorised support staff can restrict a guardian account across ExplainMe families when investigating abusive, unsafe or otherwise inappropriate use. We keep a limited enforcement record: a coded account identifier that can still be linked to the account, restriction status, reason category, case reference and timestamps or technical versions needed to prevent old credentials from being reused. Investigation details belong in the restricted support case, not in automated alert emails.

A restriction can prevent guardian sign-in, session renewal and notifications. It does not automatically delete family data, cancel an Apple subscription, disable existing linked-child reporting or notify family members. Reinstatement requires fresh guardian sign-in; previously invalidated credentials are not restored. The app cannot recall information already viewed, copied or sent.

Contact support@explainme.co.uk to ask about a restriction or request a review, including if you cannot sign in or the family account has been deleted. Do not email a child’s private reflection or conversation answers as part of a routine review request.

Advertising and tracking

ExplainMe does not use your data for third-party advertising tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Data retention

Retention depends on the purpose and type of record:

  • Deleting a guardian account does not erase a family that still has another active guardian. When the last active guardian leaves, the family is scheduled for deletion and linked-device access is revoked. Family data becomes eligible for automatic erasure after a 90-day waiting period. Erasure removes family profiles, saved check-ins and reflections, statistics, subscription records, device registrations, safety assessments, reports and AI permission records. Processing can require more than one purge run; the waiting period is not a promise that every record disappears at exactly 90 days.
  • Support reports are retained for 90 days from submission unless removed sooner with family data. Reviewing or resolving a report does not restart that period.
  • Current AI permission decisions and their date/version are kept with the family until family erasure. Withdrawal updates the current decision; it does not erase previously processed requests.
  • Limited enforcement records are stored separately from family data and currently have no automatic expiry. They can remain after family erasure and after reinstatement to prevent account deletion or reuse of old credentials from bypassing a restriction. You can contact support to request review of their continued retention.
  • Our production family-data database currently has a seven-day backup recovery window. Erasure from the live database does not immediately remove every recoverable backup copy.
  • Operational audit records and service-provider records have separate retention arrangements; deleting a family does not itself erase every operational or provider record.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time
  • Guardians can request account deletion in Settings > Account & Family > Delete My Account, or contact support if they cannot access the app
  • You can contact support if you have questions about your data
  • You can manage your subscriptions through your Apple account and App Store settings

Children’s information

Guardians manage family setup and child profiles. Children can also use a paired device, and eligible teens can use Teen Trust. Guardian involvement does not make all teen content guardian-visible: private reflections, optional transient AI inputs and support reports have the separate handling described above. Children and guardians can contact support with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete personal information; we consider the request and applicable rights, rather than assuming one family member can access another’s private information.

Privacy requests and complaints

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of personal information, obtain a portable copy, or object to its use. You can withdraw EM AI permission using the controls described above. Contact support to make a privacy request, including if your account is restricted. Some requests may require us to verify identity and consider other people’s privacy or legal obligations.

If you are concerned about how we use personal information, contact us. In the UK you can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.
