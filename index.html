Who is ExplainMe for?+

ExplainMe is for families who would like a little help with emotional communication. Children can use guided check-ins with guardian involvement. Teens on eligible linked profiles can also use guardian-enabled Teen Trust.

Families include people with different ways of communicating. You don’t need a diagnosis to use ExplainMe.

Does my child need their own iPhone?+

No. You can use Child Mode together on the guardian’s iPhone, with a parent PIN protecting the guardian area. Or you can pair a separate supported device with your child’s profile.

ExplainMe is an iPhone app. The App Store listing shows current device and iOS requirements.

What can guardians see?+

Standard child check-ins are guardian-visible. When Teen Trust is enabled on an eligible linked profile, the teen can choose to keep a check-in private or share it with their guardian.

Existing private records stay private when Teen Trust is turned off. New check-ins then use the guardian-visible path, with an explanation before entry. Private check-ins can sync to the teen’s ExplainMe account; private does not mean stored only on the phone.

Read the privacy policy for more about data handling and support reports.

Can we use ExplainMe without AI?+

Yes. Guided check-ins are available without AI. EM is an optional AI feature within Teen Trust, and both the guardian and teen must agree before AI processing. Either can withdraw permission.

Choosing not to use AI does not change the visibility of existing private records.

Is ExplainMe free?+

ExplainMe is free to download, with optional Premium subscriptions for additional child profiles, longer check-in history, weekly insights and more routines.

Plans and prices are shown in the app before you purchase. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account.

Is this therapy or emergency support?+

ExplainMe is a tool for everyday reflection and family communication. It is not therapy, medical advice or an emergency service. EM is AI, not a person, and can make mistakes.

For a concern about the app, use in-app reporting or contact support. Support reports are not continuously monitored emergency help.
